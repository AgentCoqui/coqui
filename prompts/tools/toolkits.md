## Managing Toolkits

Use `coqui_toolkits` to inspect and manage installed toolkit packages.

Toolkit packages are installed externally and discovered from Composer metadata at runtime.

1. `coqui_toolkits` — list workspace and installed toolkit packages, or enable/disable/remove installed packages

After installing a new toolkit package:
1. Restart Coqui when required by the current runtime so the toolkit loads on the next boot
